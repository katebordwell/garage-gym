The family training planner

Three people. Three sports. One plan for the garage.

Garage Gym turns scattered workout videos, busy schedules and a half-built home gym into a week everyone can actually stick to. Each person plans their own week; the app builds short sessions from the kit you own, spots when you can train together, and tells you what to buy next.

Who it's for

Households who train at home, each for their own reasons

Built for families sharing one home gym, where everyone has a different sport, a different schedule and a different idea of a good session. Garage Gym is the planning layer around the apps and clubs you already use, not a replacement for them.

The runner

"What conditioning fits around my runs this week?"

Strength and mobility sessions built from saved clips, slotted between long runs, with a quick fix when the day changes.

The cyclist

"Is the turbo free, and what strength work fits round my rides?"

Rides stay front and centre, kit bookings stop clashes, and short strength sessions fit around the bike.

The gymnast

"What can I enjoy doing around training?"

Club sessions stay fixed; short optional sessions she picks herself, and easy ways to train with family. Never an extra coach.

How it works

Plan on Sunday. Train all week.

  1. 1

    Plan your week

    Pick days, times and what to target. Add runs, rides and classes. Or repeat last week and change what's different.

  2. 2

    Get a session that fits

    Short follow-along clips, 30 seconds to 5 minutes, stacked into exactly the time you have, using only kit you own and nobody else has booked.

  3. 3

    Train, earn, buy smarter

    Finished sessions earn points. Points pick Saturday dinner and fill the family kit jar towards the next thing you all voted for.

Features and benefits

Everything the family week needs

Sessions from short clips

Your saved Instagram favourites and tagged YouTube follow-alongs, stacked to the minute. Benefit: a proper session in whatever gap the day leaves.

Knows your kit

Tick what you own and how many. Sessions only use what's free, and clashes like "bike in use by Gary" show up before Sunday's over. Benefit: no more two people, one set of dumbbells.

Train together

When two garage sessions overlap, the app offers one shared session. One person leads; anyone can leave. Benefit: more time together, without the negotiation.

Shop smarter

Everyone votes Must have, Want or Later. Each item shows why it ranks where it does: videos unlocked, clashes fixed, budget. Benefit: buy the kit that will actually get used.

Rewards that feel fair

Every finished session counts the same, whatever its length. Set your own weekly goal; opt in to the race. Benefit: the winner picks Saturday dinner, and everyone fills the kit jar.

Your look, your plan

Each person picks a theme, from retro desktop to club cycling to preppy florals, and rates clips More like this or Not again. Benefit: it feels like your app, not the family's chore list.

Every purchase unlocks something

Buy a kettlebell. Watch three new workouts appear.

Tick it off the shopping list and the library opens up straight away, so the family can see exactly what each new piece of kit adds.
